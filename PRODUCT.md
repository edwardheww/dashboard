# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single self-directed investor, reviewing Nasdaq-listed stocks during or after market hours. They know how to read a stock screener and want to scan results fast, not be onboarded. The primary task on any given screen is: run the screener and scan a small set of scored candidates to decide whether any deserve a closer look, or log/track a position they've taken.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered stock screener for Nasdaq-listed stocks, paired with a manual position tracker. API keys live server-side only (no user-facing key entry). The tool scores stocks against a set of factors, surfacing the top 3 with an overall score, a per-factor breakdown, a suggested price target, and percentage upside. Success looks like a trustworthy, fast-to-scan shortlist — not a decision made for the user, but a well-reasoned starting point for their own research.

## Brand Personality

Terminal-native, analyst-desk. Dense, precise, built for speed of scan rather than persuasion. Three words: **exact, unhurried, unadorned.** It should feel like a tool built by someone who trades, not a tool built to look fundable. Dark by default (a dim room, screens as the primary light source, extended scanning sessions) with a disciplined, restrained palette — color is reserved for signal (score bands, gain/loss), never decoration.

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid the generic AI-SaaS look: purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, hero-metric cards with a big number + gradient accent, rounded-pill everything, identical icon+heading+text card grids. No decorative motion. No copy that oversells ("Supercharge your portfolio!") — the tone is neutral and factual, like a terminal printout, not marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- **Density over whitespace.** This is a scanning tool for people comfortable with dense data, not a landing page that needs room to breathe.
- **Numbers are the interface.** Scores, prices, and percentages carry the hierarchy — typography and alignment do the work, not decorative chrome.
- **Show your work.** Every top-level score (overall, price target) must be traceable to the factor breakdown beneath it. No black-box numbers.
- **Restraint reads as confidence.** Color and emphasis are earned (signal only) — a quiet interface that trusts its data over one that performs enthusiasm.
- **Speed of scan over decoration.** Every element should make it faster to compare 3 stocks against each other, not slower.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard baseline: WCAG AA contrast minimums even within a dark, low-chroma palette; full keyboard navigability; readable type sizes despite high density (no sub-12px body text).
